Why we ask for both times →
Sleep duration is only half the picture. Research links inconsistent sleep timing — going to bed and waking up at very different times across your week, sometimes called "social jetlag" — to higher burnout and depression risk in college students, independent of how many hours you actually sleep. Bedtime and wake-up time are what let us track that; your total hours are still calculated automatically from the two. See "About the science" further down this page for the full citations.
