HOW THIS MODEL WORKS

Trained on synthetic data, not your data.

This model was trained once, offline, on a synthetic dataset of student-days generated to follow a realistic pattern — rising study hours combined with falling sleep and mood pushes the burnout probability up, with random noise mixed in so it isn't a clean lookup table. It's a {{ metrics.model if metrics else "LogisticRegression (scikit-learn)" }} model, and it never trains on your personal logs — your daily entries only ever feed the rule-based engine on the dashboard.
